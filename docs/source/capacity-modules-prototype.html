Module 2 · Overtime and headcountPerson-hour conversion

The eight-hour day converts a shortfall in pieces into a shortfall in person-hours: capacity ÷ (headcount × hours) gives units per person-hour. Where required overtime exceeds the ceiling, the balance is reported as people.
